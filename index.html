Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP Act, 2023 Compliant) • Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Rozgaar Mitra ("we", "us", "our"), a unit of GRAH LAKSHMI VIVAH (Ministry of MSME Govt. Registered Enterprise, Udyam Reg. No. UDYAM-UP-03-0139326, NIC Code 78100 - Employment Placement Agencies), operates the website rozgaarmitra.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Government Registration & Information We Collect

Rozgaar Mitra is an official MSME Govt Registered Employment Placement Consultancy. We collect:

From Job Seekers: Name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, photograph, educational qualifications, work experience, skills, resume/CV, preferred job category, location, and salary expectations.

From Employers/Consultants: Company name, industry, website, logo, description, contact details, and job posting details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your candidate profile
  • To match job seekers with relevant private job vacancies
  • To allow verified recruiters/consultants to review job applications
  • To send job alerts, application status notifications, and updates
  • To comply with Indian legal obligations including the DPDP Act 2023 and MSME guidelines

3. Data Sharing & Protection

Your profile and resume are shared with employers/consultants only when you apply for a job. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data is stored on secure cloud servers with industry-standard encryption.
