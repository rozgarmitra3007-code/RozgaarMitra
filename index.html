Privacy Policy

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP Act, 2023 Compliant) • Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Rozgaar Mitra ("we", "us", "our") operates the website rozgaarmitra.com (the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our Platform.

1. Information We Collect

From Job Seekers: Name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, photograph, educational qualifications, work experience, skills, resume/CV, preferred job category, location, and salary expectations. Login credentials are stored in encrypted/hashed form.

From Employers/Consultants: Company name, industry, website, logo, description, contact details, and job posting details.

Automatically Collected: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your candidate profile
  • To match job seekers with relevant private job vacancies
  • To allow verified recruiters/consultants to review job applications
  • To send job alerts, application status notifications, and updates
  • To comply with Indian legal obligations including the DPDP Act 2023

3. Data Sharing & Protection

Your profile and resume are shared with employers/consultants only when you apply for a job. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data is stored on secure cloud servers with industry-standard encryption.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@rozgaarmitra.com.
